Quick troubleshooting

The extension cannot find my conversations

Open https://chatgpt.com, make sure you are signed in, keep that tab open, and click Load conversations again.

A deletion task paused

Keep Chrome and a signed-in ChatGPT tab open. If Chrome was fully closed, the saved queue should resume after Chrome starts again and the ChatGPT tab is available.

Some deletions failed

Open Task status to view the failure reason. Temporary rate limits trigger a cooldown. Authentication or website-interface errors may require reloading or signing in again.

The list still shows a deleted conversation

Reload the conversation list from ChatGPT. The website may also need to be refreshed before its sidebar reflects the latest state.

简体中文帮助

如果扩展无法读取对话，请打开并登录 https://chatgpt.com，保持该标签页开启，然后重新点击“读取对话”。Chrome 完全退出时，保存的队列会暂停；下次启动 Chrome 并打开已登录的 ChatGPT 标签页后可以继续。

提交问题时，请勿公开对话标题、access token、账户信息或其他私人数据。
